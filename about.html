📖 Our Story

Born from a Love of Books & Adventure

Plush Education, LLC was founded with a single, powerful idea: what if children could fly around the world on a book? That question became the spark behind Rohi Kids™ — a brand where young learners embark on incredible journeys with Rohi, Dylan, Aya and Dr. Plush.

We write children's books about science experiments, cultural exploration and the kind of adventures that fire up a child's imagination. From the streets of Philadelphia to the ancient temples of Egypt, the cherry blossoms of Japan to the ruins of Greece — every book in our series opens a new window on the world.

At PLUSHTECH™ Headquarters, the team helps history come alive through hands-on learning, shared values and the silly STEAM brilliance of Dr. Plush. We believe that learning should never feel like a chore — it should feel like the greatest adventure of your life.
